SIP Exit Discovery Engine

Reads what people write about their SIPs in public (app reviews, Reddit, YouTube, forums) and tags why a first-year SIP stops being active, using a fixed codebook. Try it on any post below. The Golden set tab checks it against two human labellers.

Not investment advice.This is a research tool. It tags text and never recommends funds.
Pre-filled with a real Play Store review pattern. Paste anything.

Is the AI as consistent as people are?

20 items were drawn from the corpus and labelled blind by two people (us, and a finance expert not involved in the build). Running the check tags the same 20 items now and compares. The fair bar for the AI is the humans' agreement with each other: if two people disagree on a field, that field can't be trusted from anyone.

What people say vs. what people who stop describe

Advice-givers explain drop-off as returns and market nerves. People describing their own exit mostly describe broken payments and orders, and money they needed in a month the SIP couldn't bend.

Up to 3 reasons per item, so shares add up to more than 100%. Counts show what is talked about, not how often it happens. Play Store reviews over-represent platform failures.
